## 🛠️ Projects

### Active

- **[ZotLit](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit)** ([Site](https://zotlit.aidenlx.site) / [Discord](https://discord.gg/CpVTHcReAe))
  Your Zotero research desk in Obsidian: browse and query your library, read and annotate PDFs, take literature notes, cite as you write, and export to Word with a formatted reference list. Sole author and maintainer since 2022. Version 2 was a ground-up rewrite released in August 2026; version 2.2 (October 2026) added ZotLit Query.

- **[Media Extended](https://github.com/aidenlx/media-extended)** ([Site](https://mx.aidenlx.site))
  Transform your Obsidian into a powerful video note-taking tool 🖇️🗂️⏯️. Sole author and maintainer since 2021.
